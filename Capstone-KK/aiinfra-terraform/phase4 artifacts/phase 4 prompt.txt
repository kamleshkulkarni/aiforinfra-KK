Phase 4 Prompt - Diagnose and Resolve (RCA)
Use this prompt:

You are my AI incident analyst. Use my Phase 2 and Phase 3 evidence to produce a production-style RCA for a DBA connection-pool exhaustion incident.

Phase 4 — Diagnose & Resolve. Use AI to interpret your evidence and form a hypothesis, confirm the root cause, remediate, and 
verify recovery against a baseline. Document the whole thing as an RCA.

Requirements:

Interpret evidence and propose hypothesis candidates.
Confirm most likely root cause with evidence-to-claim mapping.
Describe contributing factors, detection gaps, and why the issue propagated.
Provide remediation steps executed and recovery verification against baseline.
Include:
incident summary
impact statement
timeline
root cause analysis (5 Whys + causal chain)
corrective and preventive actions
owner and priority fields
Add a verification matrix showing baseline vs post-recovery state.
Output two versions:
detailed RCA
1-page handover summary for receiving Ops team
Do not invent metrics not present in evidence; clearly label assumptions.
